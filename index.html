03 / The thinking behind the code.

The interesting part
is the tradeoff.

Correctness, isolation, and performance rarely come for free. These are some of the questions behind the systems I build.

01   Where does a tenant boundary belong?

Schema-based and row-level isolation have different costs. My production experience includes schema-based isolation; the public scaffold explores row-level isolation. The choice shapes queries, provisioning, and migrations.

02   What does “online” really mean?

A socket may disappear without a clean disconnect. Heartbeat timeouts help identify stale sessions, while cross-node updates let connected clients learn about presence changes.

03   How should agent workloads be separated?

CrewOS explores tenant-specific Celery queues and separated domain, application, infrastructure, and API layers. Queue routing makes workload boundaries explicit.
